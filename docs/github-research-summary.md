[The implemented diagnostic](confidence-feasibility.md) reports only impossibility or 'not ruled out', preserves the old model/default and requires no raw data. ## What we will not import

Frame Control's reported challenge-tuned broadcast weights/timing shifts are unsuitable evidence/settings for this project. GTT's broader pipeline includes match-local fitting even though its degradation harness is label-blind. BallRadar and MIDAS lack project-wide licences in the inspected trees. BroadTrack's implementation has restrictive terms. TrackNetV3's badminton inpainting result does not establish football recovery. A smooth or completed trajectory must remain explicitly inferred rather than become a fresh observation/control anchor.

## Next experiment and stopping conditions

The confidence guard is implemented and reviewed; the empirical confidence-removal comparison is documented separately. Next define one input-only stress harness and one small visibility-aware state/boundary/contact hypothesis. Fix the representation, calibration population, decoding units, policy grid and comparison budget before evaluation. Preserve the currently opened SkillCorner/DFL matches as seen development; another repository's split of those matches is not a fresh holdout. Obtain a separately licensed and sufficiently supported final cohort before making a new generalization claim.

Compare real possession spells, per-team newly accepted agreement, raw hidden-opponent recovery, false splits and risk/coverage by match and gap. Stop promotion when evidence/support fails. The observed simple-rule agreement of roughly53–75% is a reason to seek better signal, not a reason to lower the90% target. New detector/camera work is a separate externally validated observation project; current unqualified OCR mappings do not authorize cross-camera fusion.
