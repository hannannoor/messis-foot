# Independent audit of the work, stage 2

7 November 2026. I compared the separately supplied factual inventory with my stage 1 assessment. “Reported” below means the packet records the result. I did not reproduce it or inspect implementation, match assets or predictions.

## Verdict

**The cheapest missing comparison is the scorer's own carry-forward rule.** It can answer the participant's zero-undecided ambition and isolate the harder question of whether a model actually recovers additional possessions. The team has evidence that missing-ball rows contain usable player observations, that some external models change many frame labels, and that agreement between two frozen methods deserves a fresh test. It has little evidence that the expensive motion study recovered football possessions missed by simpler methods. It also has no cited demonstration that the local inputs preserve team identity, observation freshness and clock semantics well enough for those external findings to transfer.

## The zero-X comparison

The target's published graded mask consists of two known contiguous halves. Inside each half, explicitly carry the last decided H/A/D through X and backfill a leading gap from the first decision. Where a whole half has no decisions, D creates zero H/A spells, as does an all-X half. Leave X outside the graded scope if that is the intended meaning of “zero undecided.” On this published full-match mask, the resulting file has **zero graded X and exactly the original spellF1**, by construction. It supplies a transparent fallback and no new observations. Preserve the original uncertainty counts as diagnostics. MacroF1 can change either way. Changing X into a wrong team call adds a false positive for that team.

Use synthetic sequences to verify this property against the official scorer, including a leading X, a D anchor and a half boundary. For external experiments, generate the carry comparator using only structural periods available at inference. SkillCorner's unknown reference regions split its grading runs and can change the scorer's fills. Feeding reference gaps into the inference algorithm would give it unavailable information. Ask how many further same-team spells a proposed model matches or loses **against carry**. A method can answer more uncertain frames while doing worse at that comparison, as the direct temporal result illustrates. ## Where effort appears misplaced

1. **The gated search was structurally unable to change a prediction.** Confidence feasibility and eligible sample counts belonged before the grid and frozen test. The post-hoc feasibility CLI can prevent recurrence. The training and adaptation work may still support other policies, but running further score regressions on identical predictions cannot establish quality. The project-defined 90% side-specific agreement, 5-point coverage gain and zero declines per validation match can be secondary diagnostics. Simultaneously enforcing them can exclude a better spell detector. The 20-event support requirement is sensible for a claim about hidden opponents. When that support is absent, narrow the claim.
2. **The experiment concentrated on a subset that omits major failure modes.** Training sampled only baseline-X rows with known H/A. Hard team anchors protect false current decisions as effectively as true ones. D and other boundaries can lock in premature stoppages or restarts. The packet counts 56,016 missing-X rows in gaps over ten seconds. A policy confined to full anchor gaps of two seconds cannot address the interior of longer anchor gaps. Missing-ball and anchor gaps need a measured cross-tab before estimating exactly how much was excluded.
3. **Camera synchronization and repeated engineering checks have reached diminishing returns.** Dense OCR found zero qualified cross-camera links. That run establishes a negative feasibility result. Loosening the limit to force fusion would need independent validation. Retain the clock work if reusable, but put the next units of effort into the tactical feed, where frame alignment and tagging are already documented. The 273 tests, five skips, regression receipts, provenance and source inventories improve engineering confidence. Their count cannot substitute for a single valid comparative spell result. The packet does not establish that individual tests were unnecessary.
4. **An inexpensive provided signal has no reported incremental test in the selected route.** Smart Tagging offers team-context and restart evidence at known tactical times, particularly relevant to D→H/A. Some tags describe defending teams and every throw-in lacks a team attribution. Test code semantics and conflicts on synthetic fixtures, then assess incremental recovery on licensed, semantically comparable external data if available. If comparable tags do not exist, report that limitation. Copying phase labels, treating a restart interval as an exact whistle or manually validating local output against video would invalidate the inference.

## What I would do next, in order

1. **Settle target input semantics and reproducibility.** Verify team mapping against machine-readable metadata or documented team identifiers. Test row offsets, units, period resets and coordinate freshness with automatic checks and synthetic fixtures. Quantify how often the reprojection-error gate clears valid pitch-space ATD and whether each consumed feature actually requires image geometry. Trace the one selected production entry point to establish whether legacy fixed-frame/manual branches are reachable. Run the official validator and compare hashes from two clean regenerations. Private PR and isolated copies do not demonstrate the public, inspectable final repository required for submission.
2. **Run an affordable, discriminating external comparison.** Freeze the incumbent, explicit carry, a simple endpoint comparator and the reported agreement-only rule before opening any new evaluation data. Include the temporal argmax as a useful known failure comparator if inexpensive. Seek a small independent set with actual H/A/D reference, short turnovers, dead-ball boundaries and automatic observation gaps. Report per-match spellF1, matched/spurious/missed spells at each IoU, split/merge and wrong-team errors, plus macroF1 and gradeable support. Stratify by gap duration and presence of a visible ball. Use paired games or spells for uncertainty. The observed .0095 SkillCorner gain and seven positive games justify this test. They cannot specify its result. A fixed universal margin derived from the scorer's typical full-match noise estimate would be inappropriate.
3. **Let the error distribution choose the next change.** If the major losses are missed stoppages/restarts, test dead/alive and restart evidence before adding H/A features. If they are gratuitous switches, test a simple sequence decoder or persistence penalty against carry. If they are true hidden changes of team or bad hard anchors, test allowing a supported new spell or correcting an existing anchor, and measure newly recovered spells against false splits. On a fresh cohort with scant events, give event counts and an inconclusive verdict. As the 17 November deadline approaches, contract checks and reproducible packaging remain worthwhile even if a new cohort is unavailable.

## Facts needed to verify the open claims

- Which specific metadata fields identify home and away, and how does tactical ATD represent stale or extrapolated coordinates? Please provide the aggregate outcomes of automated invariance, freshness and geometry-gate checks.
- Does the existing matched-budget endpoint comparison include the scorer-equivalent full-half carry policy? What are the per-match spell counts, same-team matches at each IoU, D-class F1 and split/merge counts for carry, incumbent and agreement-only on external data?
- How were full-provider DFL H/A/D labels derived, including dead-ball intervals and their edges? What fraction of natural external gaps combines absent ball, similar player visibility, long gap and a known reference for the whole interval? Which such cases would each model change? Aggregates suffice.
- Has the exact submission entry point been regenerated twice from clean installs, with equal CSV hashes, a passing standalone validator, and evidence that the legacy manual options and fixed interval do not participate? Which test skips concern this route? What is the delivery plan for an inspectable public repository and technical document?

The supplied record supports keeping the loader fix, source provenance, leakage controls and simple frozen comparators. It supports treating agreement-only as a candidate. A private-score improvement, an illegal use of the legacy paths, and a verified target team mapping would each require evidence the packet does not contain.
